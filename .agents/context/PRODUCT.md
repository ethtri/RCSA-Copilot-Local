# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Bank assessment owners, independent risk reviewers, control assurance, management decision owners and GRC administrators. They review evidence and record clear decisions in a desktop assessment workflow.

## Product Purpose
Demonstrate an AI-first risk and control assessment that feels realistic and buildable. Prepared suggestions must be useful, editable and traceable to local synthetic evidence. Human judgment remains explicit.

## Brand Personality
Calm, capable, precise. The owner requested a premium enterprise experience and plain English, with complex analysis available on demand.

## Anti-references
Cramped text blocks, narrative-heavy screens, generic filler, unsupported AI certainty, invented operating protection and visual decoration that obscures the task.

## Design Principles
- Show useful prepared work and the next decision clearly.
- Separate confirmed facts, owner drafts, proposals and future work.
- Keep the default screen simple; reveal evidence and method on request.
- Use consistent typography, spacing and interaction patterns.
- Preserve the existing green identity and source traceability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Preserve keyboard focus, visible field labels/errors, narrow reflow, named table scroll regions and reduced-motion support. Do not claim formal conformance without appropriate testing.

These facts are captured from the owner's instructions and docs/ux/DEMO_EXPERIENCE_DESIGN.md.
