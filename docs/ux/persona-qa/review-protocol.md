# Independent bank-leader persona QA

Requested by the owner on November 14, 2026, after the product refinement. These are simulated leadership perspectives supplied by independent agents; they are not interviews with bank executives or a regulatory/compliance certification.

Three fresh agents received the same product boundary and separate role briefs, without the development conversation or each other's findings:

- Head of Payments Operations: practical first-line work, mappings, risk response and follow-through.
- Head of Operational Risk: evidence applicability, human challenge, risk conclusions and review traceability.
- CRO / executive committee sponsor: decision usefulness, presentation quality, visible AI value and executive reporting.

Each agent operates the rendered app in its own isolated Chrome session, inspects screenshots and explores an alternate path. Findings must identify route, steps, observed behavior, business implication and a small corrective recommendation. Observations, inferences and preferences are distinguished. No real backend, live AI or production access is expected.

The common scorecard uses 1–5 for clarity, credibility/rigor, AI value, visuals and leadership-demo readiness. Scores are subjective persona assessments, not a measured user-research result. Severity is P1 for a demo credibility blocker, P2 for a material issue, and P3 for a preference. Verdicts are Ready, Ready with fixes or Not ready.

Application code and fixtures are read-only for this review. Each agent writes a separate report and evidence folder. The primary agent consolidates duplicate findings and checks the supporting evidence. Source fingerprints are compared before and after to verify this was a review rather than a hidden implementation pass.
