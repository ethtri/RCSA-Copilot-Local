# Independent RCSA-practice review — November 14, 2026

**Verdict:** sound assessment foundation, with three priority refinements to strengthen the banking story. This review covers both first and second line. It is research-backed product challenge, not compliance certification or a claim that one RCSA method is universally required.

An independent RCSA expert reviewed the plan, fixtures and current workflow against the primary sources below. The lead agent also opened the cited sources and checked the relevant passages. Recommendations remain proposed follow-ups; this research did not implement new control, response or review fields.

## Priority findings

| Priority | Current experience gap | Smallest elegant demo enhancement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High: owner control conclusion | New mappings require an explicit coverage judgment, but Confirm controls mainly acknowledges prepared material. The owner's overall design, operation and combined-protection conclusions are not equally visible. | Add one short editable control conclusion above confirmation, with optional Design / Operation / Combined coverage detail. Keep reply sufficiency distinct. Review agreement leaves testing and management disposition open. |

Basel assigns first line responsibility for assessing controls and second line an independent view of material risks, control design/effectiveness and tolerance. It also distinguishes self-assessment, assurance and mitigation. These principles support the proposed refinements; they do not prescribe these UI choices. [Basel ORR 10.8–10.9, 10.44, 10.58–10.61](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-consolidated-guidelines/module/orr/10).

## Additional findings and optional depth

- **Reconcile supporting risks with new intelligence.** Details and cutoff risks start confirmed, while PI-12 and QI-04 introduce unresolved evidence gaps. Add an owner qualification or New evidence needs review state. An unchanged rating remains possible after consideration; do not automatically increase it.
- **Clarify scope and refresh trigger.** An expandable scope card should identify standard/urgent routes, included process steps, handoffs, dependencies, owner and assessment horizon. Show the procedure change and near miss as refresh triggers alongside the scheduled cycle. A compact cause → event → consequence explanation would sharpen each risk.

OCC's US supervisory handbook discusses candid risk assessment, emerging risks, inherent/residual assessment and more frequent assessment when warranted. Its frequency guidance should not be presented as a universal quarterly RCSA rule. [OCC Corporate and Risk Governance, printed p. 45](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/corporate-risk-governance/pub-ch-corporate-risk.pdf).

- **Optional service-outage scenario.** Show what verification unavailability near cutoff could mean for the payment journey, customer consequences and evidenced workarounds. Separate a hypothetical longer interruption from the recorded short outage, and service impact tolerance from the RCSA score threshold.

The FCA's November 11, 2026 publication emphasises demonstrated present capability, dependencies, evidence and remediation validation. It concerns UK operational resilience and explicitly introduces no new requirements. Use it as adjacent practice supporting optional scenario exploration. [FCA: Operational resilience—demonstrating resilience in practice](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/good-and-poor-practice/operational-resilience-demonstrating-resilience-practice).

## Preserve what already works

Keep the full inherent/control/residual flow; evidence limited to its tested population and period; human validation of dependency findings; explicit response creation and attestations; preserved submissions and reviewer feedback; and distinct current risk, open actions, review completion and management disposition. No arbitrary effectiveness percentages or automatic AI ratings are needed.

## Recommended sequence and delivery boundary

1. Incorporate the three high-priority refinements within the existing Controls, Response and Review screens.
2. Reconcile supporting-risk qualifications and make scope visible within the approved research/new-evidence moments.
3. Retain outage exploration as optional detail.
4. Rehearse both roles and independently verify that recorded judgments, qualifications, versions and reporting reconcile.

Use prepared records, wording alternatives and local state only. No live systems, real approvals, external messages or backend work is needed. The core wire-transfer refresh should remain approximately 8–10 minutes. These are proposed next-iteration items, not completed features.
