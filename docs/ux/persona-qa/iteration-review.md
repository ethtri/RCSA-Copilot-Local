# Independent RCSA review of the intelligence iteration

Reviewed November 14, 2026. **Verdict: Ready for the scripted demo.** No remaining blocker was observed in the targeted factual, ownership and version-awareness checks below. This is an independent product/domain review of synthetic demo behavior, not a production assurance or compliance certification.

## What was reviewed

Read-only review covered `research.js`, `research.css`, `research-demo.json`, the shared assessment content, and the assessment, control, response, model, application, version and report integrations. I also used fresh isolated installed-Chrome contexts through the repository's existing Playwright module. The user's browser tab was not used or changed. I made no application edits; this QA note is the only file written by this reviewer.

The event reconstruction keeps the supervisor-note join provisional because its payment identifier is missing. It exposes the attributes supporting the proposed match, does not infer who amended the contact record, and does not count related records as multiple incidents. The claim analysis distinguishes evidence supporting an intercepted event from the broader uncertainty about consistent urgent-route protection. Repeated references to WT-25 do not become independent tests.

The research pack bounds its search coverage and distinguishes a curated public reading pack from live research. The response comparison separates detail accuracy from independent identity verification. The longer outage is explicitly hypothetical and does not supply an automatic probability, forecast or risk rating.

Owner control conclusions are now visible, editable and recorded on confirmation. Supporting-risk conclusions acknowledge PI-12 and QI-04 without forcing an automatic rating increase. The response editor visibly separates the protective measure, its owner/date/implementation status, and the evidence needed to validate protection. A recorded implementation status is not presented as proof of effectiveness.

## Independent browser checks

- Receiving SO-19 against submitted version 1 preserved every risk record and submitted snapshot. The source receipt recorded its original affected version and did not change a rating or conclusion.
- In second line, the owner-revision action was unavailable. First line explicitly started the revision, edited the proposed control conclusion, reconfirmed controls and residual risk, reviewed and saved the response, renewed its attestation and submitted version 2.
- The incorporated-evidence view retained submitted version 1 as its baseline, displayed the actual owner-edited conclusion, identified incorporation in version 2, and offered no repeated-use action. The original snapshot and all residual ratings remained unchanged.
- An empty independent second-line assessment view prevented completion. Returning the assessment kept the review open and preserved all risk records. Agreeing with the qualified assessment recorded the reviewer rationale; the report retained that rationale and the distinction between implementation, effectiveness evidence and reassessment.
- Clearing the owner control conclusion, exploring its evidence and returning left the field visibly empty. Its default did not silently reappear over an empty saved draft.
- No page errors or failed local asset responses were observed in these isolated flows. All owned browser contexts were closed.

## Findings resolved during review

The first implementation compared new evidence with the latest snapshot, which would have lost the originally affected baseline after resubmission. It now records and resolves `evidenceBaseVersion`. Incorporated, in-draft and pending states have distinct labels and actions; the final comparison uses the owner's actual edited wording.

Default supporting-control statements originally embedded a Moderate risk band. They now describe the evidence qualification without hard-coding a rating that could contradict an edited assessment.

The control-conclusion renderer originally used a truthy fallback for an empty field. A nullish fallback now preserves an intentionally cleared draft. The incorporated-evidence side and footer also no longer tell a submitted owner that the wording is still awaiting use.

## Limits

These checks targeted the new intelligence and RCSA ownership story, including one complete evidence-to-revision-to-submission path and both independent-review outcomes. They did not repeat every older assessment/mapping combination, every viewport or a full accessibility audit; the root agent's separate regression and layout checks cover those areas. Public-practice references remain contextual, with the FCA material correctly identified as UK operational-resilience observations rather than a universal RCSA prescription. All records, analysis and source-connection behaviors remain scripted local content.
