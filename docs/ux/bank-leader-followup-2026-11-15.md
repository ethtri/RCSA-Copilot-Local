- AI proposals show the real prior inherent rating and bank-defined labels. New risks have no fabricated history.
- Adding a known unimplemented improvement retains evidence-backed residual advice, gives no current protection, and changes the next step to implementation/testing. Changed current controls invalidate stale proposals.
- Reply assistance answers testing-scope and rating questions with the submitted evidence, including accepted limited urgent tests. Prepared sample records are not misclassified as tests merely because they contain “sample”.
- Added retry/recovery risks receive relevant source-backed insights in both stages. Owner-defined or materially changed scenarios retain conservative fallbacks.
- Applying a valid suggestion explicitly retains its sources for the new revision. Failed multi-field application is atomic; earlier submitted sources and snapshots remain unchanged.
- Supporting carried-forward assessments require explicit owner review for this cycle. The review dialog shows the actual scores, explanations, control conclusion and new evidence; acknowledgement keeps those judgments and retains the sources.
- Reviewer decisions clearly cover all included risks in the exact submitted version. The full-report link selects that version. A new owner reply clears any previous sufficiency answer; the reviewer makes the choice explicitly.
- Normal product pages hide role-switch shortcuts. Presenter perspectives remain available only through the rehearsal query. This is demo behavior, not production authentication.
- Draft response ratings are labeled unconfirmed and link back to unfinished control review. Planned work cannot lower the current rating.
- Main insight cards use event counts and payment volumes; detailed rate calculations and source limits stay in drawers. Related research is risk-specific and qualified. Official IC3 BEC totals and public continuity references were checked by the copy reviewer.
- Reports explain the largest confirmed rating movement, owner rationale and next accountable work. Dates are readable and the suggested summary is separated into short paragraphs. Saved owner summaries and editions are preserved.
- Normal connections show configurable settings, with delivery mappings collapsed and distinct from assessment review. Diagnostics remain in presenter mode.
