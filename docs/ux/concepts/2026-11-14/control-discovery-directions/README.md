## Human decision behavior

Confirmation records the relationship only. Control conclusions and risk ratings stay unchanged. Add qualification records a qualified finding, including uncertainty; it does not silently confirm the relationship. A person can identify another source, dismiss the interpretation, or record insufficient information. These behavioral requirements accompany the concept images even where the initial screenshot shows only the collapsed entry point.

Both quoted sources are synthetic and effective October 5, 2026. The proposed relationship is that callback verification and supervisory approval consult the same customer-contact record. It is not a claim of control failure.

## Review focus

Choose the strongest interaction architecture and visual voice before extending the approach across the app. A prioritizes presentation clarity, B emphasizes relationship discovery, and C emphasizes inspectable evidence. Typography, responsive details, and focused follow-on states can then be refined in the selected direction.
